About Me

Hi! I'm Shinnosuke Yagi. I hold a B.S. in Data Science from Stanford University and am currently pursuing a coterminal M.S. in Statistic. My hometown is Kobe, Japan, where I spent most of my life up to high school. I am a member of the Palacios Lab at Stanford, where I am working on validating a novel distance metric for phylogenetic trees that could improve methods for detecting selection. My broader interests lie at the intersection of statistics/data science with environmental science, finance, and sports.

Outside of academics, I'm a performing member of Stanford Taiko (Japanese Drumming Ensemble) and an academic chair for SJEC (Stanford Japan Exchange Conference). I also enjoy reading books, watching baseball, and playing turn-based strategy games.
