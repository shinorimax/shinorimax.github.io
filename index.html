03 / Background

About

I hold an M.S. in Statistics and a B.S. in Data Science from Stanford University. I grew up in Kobe, Japan, where I lived through high school.

At Stanford’s Palacios Lab, I’m validating a novel distance metric for phylogenetic trees that could improve methods for detecting selection. My broader interests connect statistics and data science with environmental science, finance, and sports.

Outside academics, I perform with Stanford Taiko, a Japanese drumming ensemble, and serve as an academic chair for the Stanford Japan Exchange Conference. You’ll also find me reading, watching baseball, or playing turn-based strategy games.
